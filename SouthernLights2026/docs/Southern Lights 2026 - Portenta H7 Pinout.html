APC220 and GPS are not on hardware UARTs. Both are routed through the DFRobot WK2132 IIC-UART bridge on I2C — see IIC-UART section below.

IIC-UART — DFRobot WK2132 (DFR0627) @ I2C addr 0x10

Both UART channels share one WK2132 chip on Wire (I2C3, ESLOV/Qwiic). DIP: A1=GND, A0=GND → 0x10.

SPI — M4 core only
